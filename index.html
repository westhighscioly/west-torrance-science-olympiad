What We Do

One team, five ways to compete

Science Olympiad events are grouped into five categories. Our roster of 35 students splits into event pairs, each specializing in two or three events they build, study, and drill all season.

Life, Personal & Social Science

Anatomy, genetics & the living world

Events like Anatomy & Physiology, Designer Genes, and Disease Detectives test deep content knowledge.

Earth & Space Science

From bedrock to the cosmos

Dynamic Planet, Astronomy, and Remote Sensing challenge students to read the planet and the sky.

Physical Science & Chemistry

The rules matter is made of

Chemistry Lab, Circuit Lab, and Thermodynamics combine hands-on labs with rigorous problem sets.

Technology & Engineering

Build it, break it, rebuild it

Boomilever, Mission Possible, and Wright Stuff reward iteration and careful engineering.

Inquiry & Nature of Science

Think on your feet

Codebusters, Experimental Design, and Ping-Pong Parachute test reasoning skills that cut across every event.

Why Science Olympiad

More than a competition — it's a launchpad

Members build lab skills, design and test physical devices, and learn to work under a ticking clock alongside a partner. Alumni carry those habits into AP science courses, engineering programs, and research well beyond high school.

  • Hands-on builds: cantilevers, aircraft, and vehicles students design from scratch
  • Test-based events that reward deep study and fast recall under pressure
  • A tight-knit team culture with peer mentoring between grades
  • Direct pathway from West High to Regional, State, and National tournaments
